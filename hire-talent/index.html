Direct hire means direct

The person works for you, not for ProAdmins.

There is no ongoing staffing layer between your company and the candidate. You make the hiring decision, employ the person directly and manage the relationship internally.

  • No monthly staffing markup
  • No EOR service from ProAdmins
  • No ongoing management fee
  • Full time is typical, with part time searches available when needed

How quickly can you present candidates?

For many administrative and operations roles, initial qualified candidates can be presented within one week. More specialized searches may take longer depending on the requirements.

Who employs the candidate?

Your company does. ProAdmins is the recruiter, not the employer. You hire the candidate directly and manage the working relationship internally.

Can you recruit part time roles?

Yes. Full time placements are typical, but ProAdmins can recruit part time administrative and support roles when that is what the business needs.

Where are candidates located?

Most searches focus on remote professionals in Latin America, particularly for US companies that value practical time zone overlap.
