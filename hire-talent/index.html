What we take care of

We do the part of hiring that eats up your week.

You should not have to become a full time recruiter just because your business needs an admin. We handle the search and the early screening so you can focus on the people who made it through.

Get clear on the role

We talk through the work, tools, schedule, experience level and what you really need this person to own.

Go find the candidates

We source across our recruiting channels with a focus on remote talent throughout Latin America.

Screen them before you do

We review experience, communication, English, tools and role specific skills before sending someone your way.

Interview them ourselves

We speak with candidates directly so the first conversation on your calendar is not their first conversation with anyone.

Run the right checks

References, background screening, software checks and practical assessments are used when they make sense for the role.

Give you the shortlist

Usually two or three strong candidates. You get options without getting another inbox to sort through.

Direct hire means direct

The person works for your company, not for ProAdmins.

Once you choose the candidate, you hire them directly. You handle payroll, management and the employment relationship. We are the recruiter who got you there.

  • No monthly staffing markup from ProAdmins.
  • No ongoing ProAdmins payroll or HR layer after the hire.
  • No managed outsourcing arrangement after the hire.
