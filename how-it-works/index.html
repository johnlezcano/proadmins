Five steps

Simple for you. Thorough behind the scenes.

The process is designed to keep your involvement focused on the decisions only you should make.

1

Tell us who you need

We start with the role, responsibilities, tools, schedule, experience level and what success should look like after the person joins.

2

We launch the search

After the engagement begins, ProAdmins sources candidates with a focus on remote professionals in Latin America who match the brief.

3

We screen and assess

We review experience, interview candidates, evaluate English and use references, background screening and role specific testing as appropriate.

4

You meet the strongest two or three

Instead of reviewing a large candidate pool, you spend your interview time with a focused shortlist that has already gone through our process.

5

You choose who to hire

You make the hiring decision and employ the candidate directly. The second half of the placement fee is due when the candidate starts, and the placement is backed by a 30 Day Replacement Guarantee under the signed engagement terms.

What happens before your interview

We want the first conversation on your calendar to be worth having.

Not every role needs the same test. Our screening is adjusted to the work you are hiring for while keeping a consistent baseline around communication, experience and professionalism.

  • Resume and work history review
  • Recruiter interview
  • English communication evaluation
  • Reference checks and background review when applicable
  • Role specific skills and software assessments
