How it works

Five steps. One less hiring headache.

You tell us what you need. We recruit, screen and narrow the field. You meet the strongest candidates and hire the person you want.

Start Your Search

The process

Simple for you. Thorough on our side.

The goal is not to make hiring feel complicated. The goal is to keep you out of the parts that waste the most time.

1

Tell us who you need

We start with the work, tools, schedule, experience level and what you want this person to own.

2

We launch the search

Once the engagement starts, we source remote candidates with a focus on professionals across Latin America.

3

We screen and assess

We review experience, interview candidates, evaluate English and run role appropriate checks before introducing anyone.

4

You meet the best two or three

You spend your interview time with a focused shortlist instead of starting from a giant applicant pool.

5

You choose who to hire

Your pick joins your company directly. The second half of the placement fee is due when they start.

Then the 30 day guarantee begins

We stay behind the placement during the first 30 days under the signed engagement terms.

Before you meet anyone

We want the first interview on your calendar to be worth having.

Screening changes by role, but the point stays the same. We do enough homework to know why we are putting someone in front of you.

Screened before you meet them

We check the things that matter for the role before putting time on your calendar.

  • Relevant experience
  • English and communication
  • Role specific skills and tools
  • References and background checks where appropriate

After the hire

No staffing layer between you and the person you chose.

The candidate is hired directly by your company. That means the relationship is simple from day one.

  • Your company manages payroll and HR.
  • Your company manages the person day to day.
  • ProAdmins remains the recruiter and backs the placement with the 30 day guarantee.

Your hire. Your team.

There is no employment layer in the middle. Once you choose someone, they join your company directly.

  • You manage the employee directly
  • You control compensation and responsibilities
  • No EOR or ongoing staffing markup
  • 30 day replacement guarantee
